Colored, Glowing Edges

This is somewhat different to the usual colored, glowing cards you may have seen before!

Building off previous concepts, this card creates a subtle colored border with glowing edges that react to your mouse position.

It uses a mesh gradient background, which is masked with radial gradients to create the edge colors. Then it's masked again with a conic-gradient that follows the direction of the pointer.

The glow increases in opacity as the pointer gets closer to the edge, creating a natural magnetic feel with multi-ring luminosity shadows.
